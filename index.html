This week at a glance

  • How things are going. Meta counted 220 leads at $3.61 each, up from 51 at $4.45 the week before, on $793.44 of spend against $226.81. Booked calls were 5, up from 2. CRM leads are not available because no lead list is connected.

  • What is next. We recommend noting which guide each booked call came from before moving budget between the two campaigns, so the money can follow booked calls and not only the cost per lead.
  • Creative ideas to consider. The free 'IRS Battle Plan' guide image brought 122 leads at $2.61. It opens 'You're not just behind on paperwork. You're in a war.' The military families image for the free Hold the Line field manual, which opens 'Military families are trained to prepare for deployments,' brought 42 at $3.97, and the headshot image with the same copy brought 20 at $6.29. Worth testing a Hold the Line image that opens on IRS notices and garnishments, since the problem-first opening brings the lowest cost leads.
