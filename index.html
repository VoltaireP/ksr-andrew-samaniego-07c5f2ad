This week at a glance

  • How things are going. Leads counted by Meta rose to 102 from 86 the week before, and cost per lead fell to $2.43 from $2.91. Ad spend was steady at $247.87.
  • What to look into. There is no CRM feed yet, so we can see guide downloads but not how many of those people go on to book a call. One ad, the battle plan image ad, brought 82 of the 102 leads, so results lean heavily on a single ad.
  • What is next. We recommend connecting the lead list to this report, so each week shows how many guide readers go on to book a call and not only how many downloaded the guide.
  • Creative ideas to consider. The battle plan image ad brought 82 leads at $2.27. It opens 'If you've got unfiled tax returns or IRS debt, you're not just behind on paperwork. You're in a war,' and offers the free IRS Battle Plan guide. The revised image ad opens softer, 'IRS problems don't usually go away on their own,' and brought 15 at $2.59. The September image ad uses that softer copy: 4 at $4.95. We recommend testing the war opening on the September image, since the direct opening is drawing most of the leads.
